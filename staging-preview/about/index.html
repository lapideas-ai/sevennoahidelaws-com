Our story

This project grew out of years of biblical study, Psalms, Torah-portion reflections, and community conversations around the idea that there are universal moral principles beneath the noise of organized religion. The mission is not to create a new religion. It is to help people find meaning, peace, and moral clarity in a way that is honest, welcoming, and useful.

What we believe about how we should teach

We believe credibility matters as much as content. So we try to always be honest about where an idea comes from:

  • • What the Bible explicitly says.
  • • What established Jewish tradition says.
  • • What later commentators add.
  • • What 7NL thinks is worth exploring, clearly marked as our own interpretation.

Our relationship to Judaism

We deeply respect Judaism, Torah scholarship, and the rabbis and teachers who have preserved and taught the Noahide tradition for centuries. We are not trying to build a new religion, and we are not asking anyone to convert. Where there are legitimate differences in interpretation, we try to say so honestly.

Sources and teachers we draw from

  • • Noahide World Center / Brit Olam — an established organization with a formal declaration pathway and rabbinic guidance.
  • • Noahide Academy — educational resources for deeper learning.
  • • Sefaria — primary-source Jewish text reference.

What “light” means to us

Our promise is simple: we help you find your light, and help you share it with the world. Light here means hope, purpose, and goodness — not a performance. The dove that appears occasionally throughout this site represents peace, the same way it has across cultures for centuries.
