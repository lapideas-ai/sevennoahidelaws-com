---

## Section: Who belongs here

> You don't need to call yourself a Noahide. You don't need to have made any declaration. You're welcome here if you're:
>
> - **Curious** — you just found this and have questions.
> - **Seeking** — you're actively working out what you believe.
> - **Learning** — you're studying these principles more deeply.
> - **Living** — these principles already shape how you live.
> - **Sharing** — you want to help someone else find this too.

## Section: What's inside

> A short, honest description of what a new member finds — no empty promises about scale, just what's actually there:
>
> - **Start Here** — a welcome space and a place to introduce yourself.
> - **The Seven Principles** — discussion and questions about the framework itself.
> - **Psalms** — shared reflections, favorites, and daily readings.
> - **This Week in the Bible** — a plain-English weekly passage and discussion, using familiar references (Genesis, not "Parsha") for anyone unfamiliar with the terminology.
> - **Q&A** — ask anything, including the hard questions.
> - **Events / Live Learning** — occasional live discussions and teaching.

## Section: Always on

> This community doesn't close. Whatever time zone you're in, whatever hour you're asking your question, someone else has likely asked it too — and the space is here for you to ask it yourself.
