## Section: What we believe about how we should teach

> We think credibility matters as much as content. So we try to always be honest about where an idea comes from:
>
> - **What the Bible explicitly says.**
> - **What established Jewish tradition says** (and where different teachers or sources disagree).
> - **What later commentators add.**
> - **What we — 7NL — think is an interesting question worth exploring**, clearly marked as our own interpretation, never presented as settled fact.
>
> We'd rather say "this is disputed" or "this is our interpretation" than make something sound more certain than it is.

## Section: Our relationship to Judaism

> We deeply respect Judaism, Torah scholarship, and the rabbis and teachers who have preserved and taught the Noahide tradition for centuries. We are not trying to build a new religion, and we're not asking anyone to convert. Where legitimate disagreement exists among Jewish authorities about interpretation or practice, we try to say so honestly rather than presenting one view as the only view.
