## 3 — Adam & Noah

**Visual:** `--stone` background (first alternating panel), a simple two-point timeline graphic (Adam → Noah) rendered in restrained line-art, not illustration.

## 4 — The Seven Universal Principles

**Visual:** `--ivory` background. Seven-card grid (responsive to a single column on mobile), each card: small icon (line-art, one color — `--peace-teal`), short label, one-line description. No photography in this section — let the seven ideas stand cleanly.

**Headline:** Seven Principles for Every Human Being

**The seven cards:**

| Principle | One-line description |
|---|---|
| One Creator | There is one G-d, and life has meaning because of it. |
| Sacred Speech | What we say about the Almighty — and to each other — matters. |
| Life | Human life is sacred and not ours to take. |
| Family | Relationships and boundaries protect what matters most. |
| Honesty & Property | What belongs to someone else stays theirs. |
| Compassion for Living Creatures | Cruelty has no place in a good life. |
| Justice | Every society needs fair courts and real accountability. |

**Disclosure line (small, beneath the grid):** These are simplified, beginner-friendly expressions of the traditional Seven Noahide Laws. Explore the full traditional formulation →

**CTA:** Explore in Depth → The Seven Principles

## Implementation notes

- Sections 7 and 9/10 carry the homepage's only two active conversion CTAs (Community, Free Site, Donate) — everything before section 7 is deliberately non-transactional, consistent with the seeker-first doctrine.
- Only **one** `--gold`-filled button should be visually dominant on the full scroll at a time in any given viewport — avoid stacking multiple gold CTAs where they'd be visible together.
- All scripture/tradition distinctions noted inline above must be preserved when this copy is finalized into HTML — see CLAUDE.md's editorial discipline section.
- This document does not set the page's `<title>`, meta description, canonical, or resolve the Seven Principles URL question — those remain HOLD per SEO-EQUITY-AUDIT.md.
